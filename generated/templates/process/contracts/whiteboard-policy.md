
# Whiteboard Policy Contract

Defines the role of the Whiteboard as a planning and coordination surface.

## 1. Role

The Whiteboard represents the **"Living Plan"**. It is the bridge between frozen Specs (Artifacts) and active Execution (Memory).

## 2. Structure

- **Objective**: Current high-level target.
- **Plan**: Structured list of steps (Draft).
- **Assumptions**: Evidence-backed assumptions awaiting validation.
- **Reference Shelf**: Quick links to relevant `templates/process/contracts/` and `registries/`.

## 3. Interaction Rules

- **Drafting**: Agents may freely edit the Whiteboard during research/exploration.
- **Committing**: Once a plan is finalized on the Whiteboard, it MUST be converted into an `implementation_plan.md` artifact for user approval.
- **Authority**: The Whiteboard NEVER overrides a committed `implementation_plan.md`.
